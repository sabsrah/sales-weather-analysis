Relationship Analysis

  • Max_temp & TotalDue: There are no two way clear relationships between Max_temp and TotalDue. The points are spread out along the entire temperature range regardless of the TotalDue value, with no visible upward or downward slope. There are no noticeable upward or downward trend. On the more cool and warmer temperature days the total sales were above $15,000. This indicates that high sales is not depended on temperature but rather other factors like promotions, the days of the week, seasonal patterns and even spontaneous and unrelated to weather.

  • Max_temp & Quantity: There are also no two way clear relationships between Max_temp and Quantity as the number if units that were sold regardless of temperature between ranges 0-1,000. Even on the highest sale days, the temperature ranged from moderate to high temperatures so again it indicated that temperature does’t directly correlate with how much gets sold.

  • Quantity & TotalDue: Only this relationship between Quantity and TotalDue show a meaning positive relationship. As the quality goes up the TotalDue also goes up. This makes sense as selling more units means more total revenues; TotalDue is quantity*price. This trend shows the biggest sales days, for example 950-1000 units sold for between $16,000 to $17,000.

Summary: Of the three variable Pairs, we can conclude that high number of sales depend on how much quantity is sold naturally. And temperature has no directly relationship depending only on weather for high sales it the number of sales.
